Rules for every screen

1Light pages, royal chrome. Content stays light; only the sidebar, headers and bottom nav carry the logo's blue gradient.
2Every logo color has a job. Royal & navy = structure, cyan = live / candidates, gold = money & watch, orange = referrals & act now, red = urgent. Each vertical keeps its color everywhere it appears.
3Bright colors fill, deep colors write. The vivid logo hues go on tiles, bars, dots, badges and the swoosh. Text on white always uses the deep version so it stays readable.
4Small doses. Color sits in 34px icon tiles, 4–8px bars and one hero card. Big areas stay white or pale blue — that's what keeps it professional.
5One swoosh per surface. Top of the page, under the app header, or the bottom of the hero — never stacked.
6Status ladder. Gold = watch, orange = act now, tomato = danger / reject, green = good, cyan = informational.
7Rupees in Indian grouping (₹4,67,745, or ₹4.8L when tight); IDs read aloud on calls always in sky blue.
